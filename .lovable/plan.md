# Badaie Website Accuracy and Premium Upgrade

## Goal
Modernize the existing Badaie website without changing its TanStack architecture, official logo, routes, or working interactions. Reposition the company around verified Construction & Contracting, Equipment Rental, Manpower Supply, Power Rental, Material Supply, and Industrial Services while removing unsupported claims.

## Content audit and corrections
- Remove unsupported homepage counters, client-satisfaction figures, years-in-business claims, workforce totals, owned-fleet claims, certification claims, and invented project cards.
- Replace the About timeline, named Managing Director content, unverified history, certification language, and superlatives with factual company information and approved strengths.
- Replace invented Projects page case studies, dates, locations, project values/specifications, and completion claims with a professional capability-focused project delivery page that makes no named-project claims.
- Retain only client names already supplied in the approved project content, while removing unsupported “leading,” “most demanding,” and long-term partnership claims.
- Rewrite Safety & Quality to describe quality-focused and safety-conscious working principles without ISO/OSHA claims, incident statistics, audit claims, or guarantees.
- Remove unverified office hours and response-time promises from Contact.
- Reframe Gallery imagery as representative service and equipment visuals, not documentary proof of completed Badaie projects.

## Homepage
- Rebuild the opening section around the approved position: “Heavy Equipment, Manpower & Industrial Services,” with Construction & Contracting clearly visible in the supporting copy.
- Provide three clear actions: Request a Quote, Our Services, and Contact Us.
- Create the requested journey in this order: core services; prominent Construction & Contracting; Equipment, Manpower, Power, and Materials; Industrial Services; factual About; Contact.
- Replace statistics and fabricated featured projects with concise capability content and direct internal links.
- Preserve existing local imagery, logo, responsive behavior, and restrained animation.

## Services and capability presentation
- Build a consistent, reusable premium service-card pattern using existing imagery and icons.
- Feature Construction & Contracting first and most prominently, covering Civil Construction, Infrastructure Works, Earth Work, Excavation, Filling, Compacting, Gravel Surfacing, and Soil-Cement Surfacing.
- Present the approved services: Support, Maintenance, Manpower Supply, Heavy Equipment Rental, Power Rental, Material Supply, Scaffolding, Welding & Fabrication, Civil Construction, and Earth Work.
- Add clear groups for manpower categories: Engineering; Supervision; Office / Controllers; Riggers; Crane / Forklift Operators; Skilled Manpower; Semi-Skilled Manpower.
- Add Power Rental categories: Generator, Tower Light, Air Compressors, and Hydro Jetting.
- Present material supply and industrial services only at the category level supported by existing company content, without brands, quantities, specifications, or guarantees.

## Equipment Rental
- Keep the existing equipment route and approved local imagery.
- Present only equipment categories already represented in project assets/content.
- Remove invented capacities, fuel types, systems, technical specifications, readiness, ownership, condition, and availability claims.
- Add direct Request Equipment and Contact actions.

## Navigation, shared layout, and design
- Preserve the official Badaie logo and brand colors while refining typography, spacing, contrast, and visual hierarchy for a serious Saudi industrial B2B presentation.
- Simplify desktop navigation labels and make Construction & Contracting easier to discover through services links and page content.
- Improve the mobile menu with larger tap targets, clear state, body-scroll handling, Escape-key support, and no horizontal overflow.
- Standardize buttons, cards, section headings, focus styles, image treatment, and restrained motion across pages.
- Update the footer service links and company statement to match the verified positioning and approved contact details.

## Contact and conversion paths
- Display the approved company name, Al Jubail address, mobile number, and email consistently.
- Add clear Contact Us, Request a Quote, Request Equipment, Request Manpower, WhatsApp, and Get in Touch paths where relevant.
- Preserve the existing front-end enquiry form behavior without implying that a message was actually delivered; clearly direct users to phone, email, or WhatsApp for submission.
- Do not show a Commercial Registration number.

## SEO, accessibility, and performance
- Give every content route unique title, description, Open Graph title/description/type, Twitter card, and self-referencing canonical/og:url using the approved site domain.
- Keep structured organization data factual and limited to verified name, address, phone, email, service area, and existing social links.
- Enforce one H1 per page and logical H2/H3 order; improve landmarks, link text, form messaging, dialog semantics, keyboard behavior, focus visibility, and descriptive image alternatives.
- Keep local images, explicit dimensions/aspect ratios, priority loading for the first visual, and lazy loading elsewhere; remove unused imports and animation code.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Validation
- Run focused tests and the project’s automated build checks.
- Use the live preview to inspect every public route and exercise navigation, mobile menu, project filters/replacement content, gallery dialog, contact links, and form behavior.
- Check desktop, tablet, and narrow-mobile layouts for overflow, overlaps, broken imagery, and touch usability.
- Check console output, rendered metadata, headings, internal links, contact links, and all remaining copy for unsupported claims or spelling issues.
